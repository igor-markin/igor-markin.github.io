minion-redmi privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

minion-redmi is a personal tool used only by its owner, Igor Markin. This policy describes how it handles Google user data.

What the app accesses

The app uses the Google Drive drive.file scope. It accesses only the Drive files that it created or that were opened with it, in its owner's account.

How the data is used

The app creates and reads these files to store the results of its owner's own document, audio and web page processing. It does not use the data for any other purpose.

Storage

The access token is stored on the owner's own device, in a file readable only by the app. Files stay in the owner's Google Drive and on that device.

Sharing

The app does not sell Google user data, does not share it with third parties, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models. The app's only Google data is its owner's own.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files can be deleted in Google Drive.

Limited Use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
